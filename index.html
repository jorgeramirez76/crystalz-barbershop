Our Story

A barbershop run by family. Treats every client like one.

Crystalz Barbershop has been a fixture in Union County for over a decade. What started as one chair in Roselle Park has grown into two thriving locations — Roselle Park and Elizabeth — staffed by some of the most respected barbers in New Jersey.

The shop is owned by Leonardo "Leo" Jaramillo and powered by the three Santamaria brothers — Joel, Mauricio, and Uriel — alongside Miguel Eliza and the rest of the team. When you sit in our chair, you're sitting with family.
